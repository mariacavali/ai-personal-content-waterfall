# Management Consultant

> Representative communication profile — a management/strategy consultant.

## Writing style
Structured and precise. Framework-led, with clear signposting ("Three things stand
out…"). Full, measured sentences. Balanced qualifiers rather than absolutes.

## Preferred tone
Calm, authoritative, advisory. Neutral confidence — persuades by rigor, not volume.

## Communication preferences
- Lead with the "so what" — the implication before the detail.
- Use numbered or clearly-segmented structure the reader can scan.
- Prefer "tends to", "in most cases", "on balance" over sweeping claims.
- Always name the trade-off; avoid hype and silver-bullet framing.

## Target audience
Executives, decision-makers, and cross-functional leaders weighing a call.

## Key messaging priorities
- Clarity under complexity.
- Evidence-based recommendations with explicit trade-offs.
- Actionable next steps, not abstractions.

## Example content
> Three factors typically separate the organizations that capture value here from those
> that don't — and only one of them is technology. The other two are governance and
> incentives, and they tend to be where the effort quietly goes to die. Start there, and
> the tooling decision gets a lot easier.
