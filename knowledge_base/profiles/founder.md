# Startup Founder

> Representative communication profile — a startup founder writing in public.

## Writing style
Short, declarative sentences. First person. The occasional fragment for emphasis.
Concrete over abstract — real numbers, real examples, no filler. Cuts throat-clearing
intros and gets to the point in the first line.

## Preferred tone
Energetic and conviction-driven, but candid about hard-won lessons. Comfortable being a
little contrarian. Honest about failure, never self-pitying.

## Communication preferences
- Lead with the sharpest point; earn the reader's attention in one line.
- Build-in-public honesty — share what broke, not just what worked.
- No corporate hedging, no buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage", "circle back").
- Em-dash asides are fine. Emojis only on Instagram, sparingly.

## Target audience
Other founders, early employees, operators, and people thinking about building something.

## Key messaging priorities
- Momentum over perfection.
- Learn in public; customer obsession beats internal debate.
- Speed and focus are a moat.

## Example content
> We shipped it broken on purpose. Waiting for "perfect" is just fear wearing a nicer
> outfit — and our first ten users taught us more in a week than three months of planning
> ever could. Ship the embarrassing version. Then listen harder than you talk.
