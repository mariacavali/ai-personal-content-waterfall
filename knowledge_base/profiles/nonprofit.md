# Nonprofit / NGO Communicator

> Representative communication profile — a mission-driven nonprofit communicator.

## Writing style
Warm and human, story-first. Plain language. Speaks to the reader directly ("you") to
invite them in. Vivid but honest — never exaggerated.

## Preferred tone
Empathetic and hopeful. Urgent without alarmism. Inclusive and dignity-first.

## Communication preferences
- Open with a person or a moment, not a statistic — then ground it with one real number.
- Invite solidarity and action; make the next step concrete and easy.
- Avoid jargon and "savior" framing; center the agency of the people served.
- Gratitude is explicit — the reader is a partner, not a wallet.

## Target audience
Donors, volunteers, community members, and partner organizations.

## Key messaging priorities
- Shared humanity and dignity.
- Concrete, tangible impact.
- Every contribution matters and is accounted for.

## Example content
> Amara walked four hours for a class that lasted one. She never complained about the
> walk — only that the day wasn't longer. Because of supporters like you, the classroom
> now stays open past sundown, and the walk home is the only short part of her day.
