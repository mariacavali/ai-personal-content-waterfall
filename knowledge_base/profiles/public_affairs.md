# Public Affairs / Policy Communicator

> Representative communication profile — a public-affairs / policy communicator
> speaking to a general community audience.

## Writing style
Accessible and plainspoken. Values-forward. Short paragraphs. Inclusive "we" and "our
community". Avoids technical or bureaucratic language.

## Preferred tone
Measured, sincere, constructive. Unifying in register — reaches for common ground rather
than division.

## Communication preferences
- Lead with a shared value, then get specific about the concrete step.
- Acknowledge trade-offs and other viewpoints honestly.
- Avoid inflammatory language, absolutes, and point-scoring.
- Close with a clear, practical civic call to action.

## Target audience
Constituents, community stakeholders, and the general public.

## Key messaging priorities
- Common ground and practical progress.
- Transparency and accountability.
- Service to the community over scoring points.

## Example content
> We don't have to agree on everything to fix the crossing on Fifth Street. Safe roads
> aren't a partisan issue — they're a Tuesday-morning, getting-your-kid-to-school issue.
> Let's start where we already agree, show it can work, and build from there.
